Playing Radical Red, the companion gets one more tab, first in the bar: `NUZLOCKE`. It answers the questions a hard run keeps asking. How much does this move do? Who wins this fight at the level cap? Which route haven't I used yet? What should I bring to the next boss?

It reads your game as you play, so there's nothing to type in. It shows only for Radical Red 4.1, in builds that carry its files.

## Its pages

- **Route**: the wild Pokémon of the place you're in (by time of day and floor), the guide's pick, and how many encounters you have left.
- **Battle**: in a battle, both sides' Pokémon with their items and abilities, the turn order, and a table of what every move does both ways. In a double battle it warns you when both foes together could knock one of yours out. Outside a battle, **Future bosses** shows every fight still ahead.
- **Next**: your next required fight, its team, your level cap, and your best answer to each of its Pokémon, trained to the cap. **Theory craft** lets you try any of your Pokémon against it.
- **Team**: your party and boxes, and the team helper (below).
- **Dex**, **Items**: every Pokémon's data, and where to find items, TMs and tutors.
- **Lines**: a turn by turn plan for the fight you're in. It's off until you ask for it, so it never spoils a fight you want to work out yourself.

## Nuzlocke or playthrough

Open the tab's own settings (the gear at the end of its page list) and pick `Nuzlocke` or `Playthrough` under `MODE`. The tab's name follows your pick: `NUZLOCKE` or `PLAYTHROUGH`.

- **Nuzlocke** tracks your encounters: which routes you've used, how many are left, duplicates, and boxes 20 to 25 as the graveyard.
- **Playthrough** keeps everything else and drops that bookkeeping. Boxes 20 to 25 count as normal boxes.

## The team helper

Know you want to use three Pokémon, but not who should join them? On the Team page, tap up to three of yours, then `Find partners`. It trains every other candidate to your level cap (your own Pokémon, and the ones you can still catch by now; in a nuzlocke only on routes you haven't used) and lists the six that add the most, with the reason: the foes of your next fights they handle where your three struggle, and the attacks your three are weak to that they take well.

## In a battle

The tab stays where it is when a battle starts, since its Battle page is its own battle view. The companion's `BATTLE` tab is still in the bar.
