The library plays DS games too: put `.nds` files in your ROMs folder (or add them with the library's `+`) and they show up next to your other games. Tap one to play.

There's nothing else to set up. The app brings its own DS start-up code, so you don't need any BIOS or firmware files.

## The two screens

On a device with two screens, like the AYN Thor, the DS's top screen is on the main display and its touch screen is on the second one. Tap or drag on the second display to use the stylus.

Want them the other way round? Press R3, or SELECT and R together, to swap them. Press it again to swap back.

On a device with one screen, the two DS screens sit one above the other.

## Buttons

The face buttons, shoulders, START, SELECT and the D-pad are the DS's own: A, B, X and Y, L and R.

## Saves

A DS game saves to its own `.sav` file, kept and backed up the same way as your other games' saves (see [Saves and backups](saves.md)). The game writes it every few seconds while you play, and again when you leave.

## The RUN tab

For some games a small `RUN TAB` button sits on the touch screen. It opens a page with your party, what you can find in the area you're in, and your next big fight. It supports Black, Blaze Black, Black 2, Blaze Black 2 Redux, Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver. It follows your battles too, in Black 2, Blaze Black 2 Redux, Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver: during a fight its Battle page shows both sides' Pokémon and how much HP they have left. Its Next page follows your progress, so it moves on to the next big fight after each win, in Black 2, Blaze Black 2 Redux, Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver: gym leaders by their badges, and in the last four the Elite Four, the Champion, your rival and the villain team's bosses too (and Kanto's gym leaders in HeartGold and SoulSilver). Tap `BACK` to close it.

## The pause menu

Press BACK (or L3) to pause. The menu has:

- `RESUME`: back to the game.
- `SAVE STATE` and `LOAD STATE`: one save state per game, saved the moment you pick it. `LOAD STATE` is greyed out until you've saved one.
- `FF`: fast forward, four times the normal speed, without sound. Holding R2 fast forwards too, for as long as you hold it.
- `HEAL PARTY`: fills every Pokémon's HP and PP and clears its status, anywhere, like a handy healing station. Only for games the RUN tab supports, and not in a battle.
- `GAME CLOCK`: shows the game's clock hour. Press right or A (or tap it) to move it an hour later, left to move it an hour earlier, for day and night events. The menu stays open so you can step to the hour you want.
- `NATURE`: pick a Pokémon in your party, then the nature you want it to have, like a nature-changing item would. Its stats change straight away to match (its level, training and HP stay the same). In Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver the nature comes from the Pokémon's hidden personality value, so that changes too, but its gender, ability and shininess stay. Black, Blaze Black, Black 2, Blaze Black 2 Redux, Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver for now.
- The endless spray (the row named after the item that keeps wild Pokémon away): off to start with, remembered for each game. While it's on, no wild Pokémon turn up in grass, caves or water (fishing still works), whatever level your lead is. Switch it off and they come back. It works in Black, Blaze Black, Black 2, Blaze Black 2 Redux, Platinum, Renegade Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver.
- `SHORTCUTS`: on by default. In a wild battle, press R to run away and L to throw your best ball (Ultra, then Great, then Poké and so on; never your Master Ball) without opening any menus: the app presses the game's own buttons for you. Trainer battles aren't affected. Only for games the RUN tab supports.
- `SWAP SCREENS`: the same as R3.
- `CLOSE GAME`: back to the library. The game's save is written first.

A save state is a snapshot of the emulator, separate from the game's own save. Save in the game as usual too.

The companion's tabs and the other GBA extras don't work with DS games yet.
