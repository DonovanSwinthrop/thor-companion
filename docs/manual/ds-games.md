The library plays DS games too: put `.nds` files in your ROMs folder (or add them with the library's `+`) and they show up next to your other games. Tap one to play.

There's nothing else to set up. The app brings its own DS start-up code, so you don't need any BIOS or firmware files.

## The two screens

On a device with two screens, like the AYN Thor, the DS's top screen is on the main display and its touch screen is on the second one. Tap or drag on the second display to use the stylus.

Want them the other way round? Press R3, or SELECT and R together, to swap them. Press it again to swap back.

On a device with one screen, the two DS screens sit one above the other.

## Buttons

The face buttons, shoulders, START, SELECT and the D-pad are the DS's own: A, B, X and Y, L and R.

## Saves

A DS game saves to its own `.sav` file, kept and backed up the same way as your other games' saves (see [Saves and backups](saves.md)). The game writes it every few seconds while you play, and again when you leave.

The companion's tabs, save states, fast forward and the other GBA extras don't work with DS games yet.
